Approach

Small team. Short loops. Products that ship.

CAM Capital is a studio and a portfolio in one. We spot problems worth solving, build focused products around them and launch them ourselves, then keep the ones people love growing.

  1. 01

    Spot

    We start from a real itch: a group chat that can't schedule a game, a playlist no one hears. If the problem is sharp, it's worth a prototype.

  2. 02

    Build

    Design and engineering sit at the same table. We ship a lean first version in weeks, on modern web and mobile stacks.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Real users, real feedback, fast. We put the product in the market early and let usage tell us what to build next.

  4. 04

    Grow

    What works gets more investment: features, polish, distribution. What doesn't teaches us something for the next launch.
